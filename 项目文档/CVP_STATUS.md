# CVP evidence status: OPEN

The local real-service experiment can support an engineering demonstration only. A deliberately weak application baseline is not proof that PgCat or PostgreSQL has the defect. No claim of upstream vulnerability, exploitation, production impact, program eligibility, or acceptance is made.

The application would still need a real authorized defensive use case, an accurate account/organization identity, and program-specific evidence of the relevant protection impact. Those facts have not been established by this synthetic lab. This candidate must not be counted as an accepted CVP case until they are independently verified.
